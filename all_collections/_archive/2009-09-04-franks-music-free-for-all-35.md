For almost a year now, I've been using the popular social networking site Twitter. Through Twitter, I've met some pretty amazing people, and I've found that we all have something in common: our love for music. For this episode, I've turned the tables and asked some Twitter friends to choose the music and talk with me about it. There's some great discussion, very cool people, and as always, great music!
