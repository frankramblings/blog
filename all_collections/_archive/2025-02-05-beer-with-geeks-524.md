Frank and Tim stretch their geek muscles to break down the teaser trailer for Marvel’s _The Fantastic Four: First Steps _(2025)! They chat about the film’s retro-futuristic aesthetic, the exciting casting choices, and why Marvel’s First Family feels like quintessential comic book fun. From Pedro Pascal’s Reed Richards to a glimpse of Galactus, they explore what makes this trailer a fantastic first look. Cheers!
