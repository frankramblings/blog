This week, the guys are joined by intrepid Whedon podcasters Tabz & Kim from the Between the Lines Studios. The discussion focuses around the panel's favorite moments and general themes from Season 2 so far, as well as a few predictions. Please note that the episode was recorded a couple of days before Dollhouse was officially cancelled, so some remarks may sound outdated. Still, the vast majority of the discussion is still totally valid...well, as valid as Echo Alert can be, anyway.
