Clark shows Lana that he has Kara’s crystal, which contains his birth mother’s DNA. </em>This Week on SHoE – Derek and Steve Glosson from Geek Out Online cover everything, from the Hollywood writers Guild strike, to episode 7×06 of Smallville, “Lara” and all the new topics and theories it has formed, including news on the Superman sequel, casting news and tons of new spoilers – we hope you enjoy this extended episode!</p>
