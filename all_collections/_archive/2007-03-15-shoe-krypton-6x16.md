<p>March 15, 2007 – <em>Lana begins having second thoughts about her marriage to Lex, and finally decides to find out the truth about Clark. After learning the truth, by way of deception, Lana promises Clark that she isn’t going to marry Lex. Lionel realizes what is going on and forces Lana to walk down the aisle. Clark, unaware that Lana knows the truth, is stunned when she marries Lex. </em>The SHoE crew is back after a week off and we’re coming’ at you with our thoughts on “Promise” the long awaited wedding episode. Graham and Derek talk about their recent travels with John Schneider as well as a few interesting things that are lining up in the SHoE universe.</p>
