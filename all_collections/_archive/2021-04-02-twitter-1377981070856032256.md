If you’ve ever wanted to pray, but weren’t sure where to begin, try this: “Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner.” 

Say it once, or repeat it like a mantra while you meditate. 

I’m praying for all of you on this Good Friday. ❤️
