Jimmy and Lois work together to uncover the truth behind Patricia Swann’s murder. Lex begins searching for the secret of Veritas with a key he found in Patricia’s pendant. Kara attempts to teach Clark to fly, but he is more focused on finding Brainiac, who attacks Lana at the Isis Foundation. In order to save Lana’s life, Kara agrees to help Brainiac. Lana is placed in a clinic, stuck in a catatonic state, while Clark and Chloe have no leads as to where Brainiac and Kara are. </em>This Week on SHoE – Derek and Steve return with another big dose of SHoE to bring you an in-depth look at the original finale for the 7th year of Smallville, “Veritas”. Not only that, full news on the Superman legal battle, news of the final 2 episodes of this season, and a look at what changed pre/post strike.</p>
