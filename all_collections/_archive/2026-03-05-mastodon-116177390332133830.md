It's here! SocialFusion is now in public beta! 🥳 

One app. Your Mastodon timeline and your Bluesky feed, together.

I built this because I got tired of checking two apps to keep up with the people I care about. If you're on both networks, this is for you.

It's written entirely in Swift for a smooth, native iOS experience.

Join the beta: https://testflight.apple.com/join/EhYZnjd2

More info: https://frankramblings.com/socialfusion
